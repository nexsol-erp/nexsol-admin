NOTES ON SENDING
- Attach the PDF for the first email only. A follow-up with an attachment is
  far more likely to be filtered.
- Send from a person's address, not info@ or sales@. Cold mail from a role
  address lands in spam and reads as a blast.
- Keep the first email under 200 words of body copy if you cut it further —
  the four points are the part to protect.
- The offer to load their own data is the strongest line here. Do not drop it
  to save space, and do not make it unless you will actually do it in a day.
