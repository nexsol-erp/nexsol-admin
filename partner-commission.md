For TradeLink247, I would **not** copy the traditional ERP model (10–20% one-time commission). Your ERP is SaaS-based, so your partner program should motivate partners to **keep customers subscribed for years**.

Below is a commission structure that is attractive to partners while keeping the business sustainable.

# Payment Terms

* Commissions are paid after customer payments are received.
* Subscription commissions are calculated on the subscription amount actually collected.
* Renewals qualify for renewal commissions while the customer remains active and associated with the partner.
* Refunds or chargebacks may result in corresponding commission adjustments.
* Service fees charged directly by partners are not included in commission calculations.

## Recommendation

One change I would make to maximize long-term growth is to **avoid offering a permanent 50% revenue share**. While attractive initially, it can significantly reduce your recurring margins as your customer base grows.

A more sustainable approach is:

* Referral Partner: **20% first year, 10% renewals**
* Sales Partner: **30% first year, 15–20% renewals**
* Implementation Partner: **40% first year, 20–25% renewals**
* Master Partner: **Negotiated individually**, based on sales targets, geographic coverage, and support responsibilities.

This structure rewards partners for acquiring and supporting customers while preserving enough recurring revenue for ongoing product development, hosting, customer support, and future AI features.
